Privacy Policy — VibeShort

Effective date: 2 November 2026  •  App package: com.vibeshort.app

VibeShort is a short-video app where users watch, record and share videos. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app stores, why, and what choices you have. If anything here does not match how the app behaves, contact us and we will fix it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, display name, username, profile photo and bio. Login is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create. Videos and profile photos you upload, comments you post and sounds you publish. Videos and photos are stored with our media provider, Cloudinary; the related text records (titles, captions, counts) are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • App activity. Your likes, follows, favourites, reposts, watch history, and the videos you hide or report — stored so the app can show them back to you and keep your feed working.
  • Wallet and rewards records (inside the app). Your coin balance, coin transactions, referral records and any withdrawal requests you submit (including the mobile-wallet account name and number you type into a withdrawal request). Withdrawals open from Rs 500 (5,000 coins) via Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer; 1,000 coins = Rs 100.
  • Advertising. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers as described in Google's own privacy policy.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a video or an account, we store the report (what was reported, the reason you selected, and your account ID) so our moderation team can review it. Your block list is stored so blocked accounts stay hidden from your feeds.
  • On your device only. Videos you download for offline watching, your app preferences (such as Data Saver) and similar settings stay in the app's own storage on your phone and are not uploaded.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera and microphone — only when you record a video inside the app.
  • Photos / videos access — only when you pick a video or photo from your phone to upload or set as a profile photo.
  • Internet — to load the feed and upload your content.

Face effects use on-device face detection (Google ML Kit). Face detection runs entirely on your phone — no face data is uploaded or stored on our servers.

3. What other people can see

Your profile (name, username, photo, bio), your public videos, your comments and your follower/following counts are visible to other signed-in users of the app. Your email address, password, block list, watch history and wallet records are never shown to other users.

4. How we use information

  • To run the app: show the feed, play videos, and display your profile and activity back to you.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and suspend accounts that abuse the app.
  • To operate the rewards system: credit coins, prevent fake or duplicate claims, and process withdrawal requests.
  • To communicate with you about your account when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication and database) and Cloudinary (video and image storage and delivery) to run the app. These providers process data on our behalf to provide those services. Their own privacy policies also apply to that processing.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, videos, comments, likes, follows and wallet records from the app's database and deletes your login. Media files already uploaded to the storage provider cannot be individually erased by the app, but after account deletion they are no longer linked to your account or shown anywhere in the app. You can also ask for deletion by emailing us (see Contact).

7. Children

VibeShort is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is controlled by authentication and server-side rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this address and shown in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
