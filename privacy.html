Privacy Policy (رازداری کی پالیسی)

Aakhri update: 18 October 2026

1. Hum kon hain

Quizistan Academy Punjab Board ke Class 9–12 students, teachers aur academies ke liye ek taleemi (educational) website hai. Hum aapki privacy ka ehtiram karte hain aur sirf utna data jama karte hain jitna website chalane ke liye zaroori hai.

2. Hum kya data jama karte hain

  • Account / Login: Is website par koi account ya login nahi hai — hum aapse email, password ya phone number nahi maangte.
  • Reviews: Agar aap "Log kya kehte hain" section me review likhte hain to hum sirf wahi mehfooz karte hain jo aap khud likhte hain — naam (optional), star rating, aur review ka text. Ye Google Firebase (Firestore) me store hota hai aur website par sab ko nazar aata hai.
  • Quiz progress: Aapki quiz practice aur theme (day/night) ki setting sirf aapke apne browser me (localStorage) mehfooz hoti hai — humare server par nahi bheji jaati.

3. Cookies aur local storage

Hum tracking cookies istemal nahi karte. Sirf aapke browser ki local storage me chhoti settings (jaise theme aur quiz progress) mehfooz hoti hain taake website behtar chale. Aap chahein to apne browser se ye data kabhi bhi saaf kar sakte hain.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase / Firestore: Reviews ka data store karne ke liye.
  • GitHub Pages: Website ki hosting ke liye.
  • Google AdSense (mustaqbil me): Jab website par ads lagenge to Google apni ad policy ke tehat cookies istemal kar sakta hai. Aap Google ad settings se personalized ads band kar sakte hain.

5. Bachon ki privacy

Ye website students (aksar 18 saal se kam umar) ke liye hai. Hum bachon se koi zaati maloomat (email, phone, address) jama nahi karte. Reviews me naam dena poori tarah optional hai — aap "Mehman" ke naam se bhi review likh sakte hain. Waldein ya teachers ko koi tashweesh ho to neeche diye gaye email par rabta karein.

6. Aapke huqooq

Agar aapne review likha hai aur chahte hain ke hum usay hata dein ya tabdeel karein, to humein email karein — hum aapki darkhwast par amal karenge.
